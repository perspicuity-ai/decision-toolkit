# Perspicuity

Version: 0.2.0. Record format: `perspicuity-work/1`.

Maintain one inspectable record from intention through authorized work and outcome review.
Use the record requirements below as the completion standard for the requested scope.
Choose any suitable analytical method.
Adapt the depth and order to the case.
Treat Understand, Choose, Act and Review as responsibilities that can repeat or overlap.

## Start or resume

Read the request and any supplied record before asking questions.
Identify the desired outcome, current choice, actual authority and next unresolved issue.
Use existing context to draft a useful starting point.
Ask for missing facts or preferences only when they could materially affect the work.
Continue useful independent work while an answer remains pending.

Read [the record guide](references/record.md) when creating, revising or resuming a record.
Create the record at the start from [the template](assets/record-template.md).
Add content as it becomes relevant.
Resume an existing record without creating a replacement identity.
Keep actual deliverables in the user's existing tools.

For a human-retained choice, help the person articulate preferences and select an approach.
For a delegated choice, inherit the principal's objectives and limits.
Use the same record when the decider changes.
Reuse existing permission when it covers the next action.
A skill invocation supplies no additional permission.

For settled work, reuse the choice and proceed to Act within its authority.
For advice or design only, stop at the requested output.
Keep the current position and next actor visible at every stop.

## Required decision basis — Understand

Read [the basis criteria](references/analysis.md) for a new or materially changed choice.
Before selection, establish a sufficient basis for the choice at the actual stakes.
Keep these elements inspectable in the record:

- The original request, chosen frame and reason that frame serves the intended outcome.
- Valued outcomes, proposed means, usable measures and relevant time horizons.
- Credible alternatives, including combined or conditional strategies where useful.
- A consequence table with evidence, judgment and uncertainty attributable to their sources.
- The preferences and accepted sacrifices that support the conclusion.
- Material unresolved questions and conditions that could reverse the conclusion.

Use concise explanations of the decision basis and inspectable work evidence.
Identify what was actually examined and how it bears on the conclusion.
Do not present this account as a transcript or proof of the model's internal reasoning.
Scale further inquiry to its likely effect on the choice.

If a missing preference could reverse the choice, locate that preference with the authorized decider.
State a conditional recommendation when the available basis supports only that conclusion.

## Required commitment — Choose

Distinguish a recommendation from an actual selection.
Record who chose, when, on what basis, and under which instruction.
Explain the accepted tradeoffs and conditions for reconsideration.

Translate the selection into a compact undertaking:

- The work, responsible actor and applicable permission.
- Constraints, resource limits and material prerequisites.
- Evidence required to accept the output.
- The expected benefit, its measure and observation source.
- The review owner, time or trigger, and response to a missed expectation.

Reuse established terms instead of asking for repeated confirmation.
Separate immediate delivery criteria from benefits that need later observation.
Keep proposed targets distinct from agreed targets.

Proceed when the choice and permission cover the work.
If the person retains a material choice, present it with its consequences and your recommendation.

## Required work evidence — Act

Perform the authorized work using available tools and applicable specialist guidance.
Keep routine planning and execution inside this conversation when practical.
Link work to its applicable choice revision.
Inspect outputs against the original criteria as work proceeds.
Correct material defects throughout the affected work within scope.

Retain material failures and unresolved effects before a retry.
Check uncertain external effects before repeating an action.
Return a changed decision basis to Understand or Choose.
Keep previously valid work and its evidence available.

Report what was performed and what was checked.
Label self-checks as self-checks.
Use independent assessment when the assignment requires it or consequential uncertainty warrants it.
Distinguish an accepted deliverable from an observed benefit.

## Required assessment — Review

Compare observed results with the original objectives and forecasts.
Identify evidence, dates, remaining uncertainty and alternative explanations.
A good outcome alone does not establish a good decision or causal success.

Continue, correct, reconsider or close the work according to the findings and authority.
If benefits remain unobserved, mark delivery and outcome status separately.
Give each material follow-up an owner, evidence source and time or trigger.
Suggest a calendar check when delayed observation needs a reminder.
Create a reminder only when authorized and supported by an available tool.
Record whether the reminder was actually created or merely proposed.
A skill alone cannot run after a session ends.

## Keep it easy to use

Present the current finding and the next consequential choice or action.
Keep detailed support in the record.
Ask questions in manageable groups that serve the next useful step.
Let the user inspect, correct, redirect or stop the work.
Assess whether the record's claims are supported.
Keep unsupported conclusions open even when every heading contains text.
Use named methods only when they help the case or the user requests them.

See [the fictional pipeline example](examples/pipeline.md) when distinguishing delivery from later benefit would help.
